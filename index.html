    How Splendor plays

    One action per turn. Nothing else.

    Your turn — pick exactly one

    • Take 3 gems of three different colours.
    • Take 2 gems of the same colour — only if that pile still holds 4 or more.
    • Reserve a card from the table or the top of a deck, and take 1 gold with it if any is left. Three reserved cards at most.
    • Buy a card from the table or from your reserve.

    Paying for cards

    • Every card you own gives a permanent bonus of its gem: a discount of one gem on every future purchase, and it is never spent.
    • Gold is a joker — each one covers a single gem of any colour.
    • Gems you spend go back to the bank for everyone to take again.

    Gold, limits and nobles

    • Gold can only be gained by reserving a card, never taken on its own.
    • You may hold 10 gems at most. Over the limit at the end of your turn, you return gems until you are back to 10.
    • A noble comes to you the moment your bonuses meet their requirement — automatically, and at most one per turn. Each is worth 3 prestige.

    Ending the game

    • When a merchant reaches 15 prestige, the round is played out so everyone has had the same number of turns.
    • Most prestige wins. On a tie, the merchant who bought fewer development cards takes it.
